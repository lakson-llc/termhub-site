Drive your coding agent from your phone.

TermHub is an SSH terminal for Android built around Claude Code, Codex, and Pi in tmux or herdr. A command dial and a shortcut bar give you the keys a phone keyboard hides, and nothing sits between you and your machine.

Why I built it

I kept walking away from my desk while an agent was halfway through a task. Checking in from my phone meant fighting the keyboard: no esc, no shift-tab, no Ctrl-C, and a tmux prefix that took three taps.

So I built the terminal I wanted. A dial for the keys you press all the time, a bar for the rest, voice for long prompts, and a notification when the agent finishes. No server in the middle and no account. It connects straight to your machine.
