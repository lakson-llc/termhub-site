TermHub

A fast, modern SSH terminal for Android.

Connect to your servers, run commands, and keep your hosts in sync - with an encrypted backup only you can unlock.

SSH terminal

A full terminal with a signature command dial, voice input, and image send - built for real work on the go.

Encrypted cloud backup

Optional. Your hosts and settings are encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know, then stored in your own Google Drive.

Your keys stay yours

SSH credentials live in your device's secure keystore and connect directly to your hosts - never through our servers.

On-device voice

Dictation is transcribed on your phone. Audio never leaves your device.
