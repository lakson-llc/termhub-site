A fast, modern SSH terminal for Android.

Connect to your servers, drive tmux or herdr, and run your favorite AI CLI like Claude Code, Codex, or Pi from a real terminal. Your keys never leave your phone.

Guides

Set up and get connected

Everything from your first connection to running a coding agent in tmux or herdr. Each guide takes a few minutes and uses real screenshots.

Getting started

Start here

Install, add a host, sign in, connect, and drive the Command Dial without the keyboard.

Reach any server with Tailscale

A private path in with no port forwarding, plus the LAN and tailnet fallback trick.

Run Claude Code, Codex, or Pi

Keep an agent alive in tmux or herdr and steer it from the dial, no keyboard needed.

Voice dictation

On-device speech to text with punctuation, straight into the terminal line.

Encrypted cloud backup

Move your hosts to a new phone, encrypted with a passphrase only you hold.

Host check

Make sure your host is phone-ready, from tmux and herdr visibility to sleep.
Why TermHub

Built for real work on a phone

Real terminal, not a wrapper

A full PTY with a signature command dial, voice input, and image send, so you drive any CLI or TUI directly.

Your keys stay yours

SSH credentials live in your device's secure keystore and connect straight to your hosts, never through our servers.

Encrypted cloud backup

Optional. Hosts and settings are encrypted on-device with a passphrase only you know, then stored in your own Google Drive.

On-device voice

Dictation is transcribed on your phone. Audio never leaves your device.
